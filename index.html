Andrejs Zavadskis: Commercial, Advertising, Editorial and Portrait Photographer Trusted by Forbes USA — from Dakar Sand to Gumball 3000 Convoys

European photographer based in Riga, Latvia — a commercial, advertising, editorial and portrait photographer from the Baltic States whose portraits run in Forbes USA and whose camera has followed the Dakar Rally through Saudi sand and five Gumball 3000 rallies across the road. Four roles, one discipline: understand what the picture has to do, then make it happen in the time available.

A founder walks in with fifteen minutes and a wall of handlers. A brand needs a campaign image that sells an idea in a single glance. A magazine wants a face that carries the headline. A rally truck the size of a house is coming through the dust and will not come back for a second try.

This is not a biography. It is a short tour of the assignments that made Andrejs Zavadskis one of Latvia's most famous photographers — and one of the most versatile in Europe.

The portrait that holds the headline

Forbes USA does not send a long brief. It sends a name, a city and very little time.

  • Thomas Plantenga, Vinted — April 2024, "Riches from Rags".
  • Paweł Chudziński, Point Nine Capital — the "Europe's Top VC" cover, also used for the Midas List Europe 2024.
  • Remon Vos, CTP — photographed in Prague; now part of The Forbes Collection on Getty Images.
  • Samir Mane — Albania's first billionaire, April 2025.
  • Alex Bouzari, DDN — August 2026.

The same editorial portraits travelled on to Forbes México, Forbes Česko, Forbes Italia and Forbes España.

Campaigns, brands, one idea per frame

Clients include Forbes USA, Nike, airBaltic, LMT, Rimi, Nordea, Swedbank and Tele2. One early commercial job was the airBaltic 2009 calendar, made with the advertising agency Base. In advertising and commercial photography the rule is simple: understand the client's objective first, then find what the subject can do for it.

A woman in a white spacesuit and gold-visored helmet looks off to the side under warm, directional light. The image is part of the advertising section of his portfolio — a staged concept frame built around one strong idea. Photo: Andrejs Zavadskis.

Sand, convoys and a summit

January 2020: the Dakar Rally in Saudi Arabia — heat, dust and machines that do not stop for the photographer. Then five Gumball 3000 rallies, with his pictures printed in the official Gumball 3000 photo book. Porsche cars on frozen lakes in Swedish Lapland. And in 2022, the summit of Kilimanjaro in Tanzania — 5,895 metres — reached while documenting the expedition.

A man in a dark uniform and cap stands with his back to the camera on an asphalt road streaked with tyre marks, while a white sports car and a line of cars wait behind him. The low, wide angle comes from the automotive section of his portfolio, the same field that took him to the Dakar Rally and the Gumball 3000. Photo: Andrejs Zavadskis.

A film set and the desert at night

In 2021 he shot behind-the-scenes stills for the Danish feature Hvidstengruppen II, partly filmed in Latvia; the Latvian Ministry of Culture used one of his frames in its official announcement, credited to him. Burning Man in Nevada's Black Rock Desert offered the opposite: no brief, no client, just dust, light and curiosity.

Playboy Latvia, 2010–2014

Four years. Fifty issues. Twenty-two of them shot by Andrejs Zavadskis — no other photographer put more issues of the Latvian Playboy on the newsstand. He joined around the third issue. One of his Latvian models was picked by a foreign Playboy edition, a first for the magazine; in total three of his models were published abroad. In May 2014 he shot the first man ever on a Playboy Latvia cover, singer Lauris Reiniks. Read his story of the 22 issues.

The cover of Playboy Latvia, Nr. 4, January 2011, with Oksana on the front. It is the earliest of the covers listed in his own account of the magazine, which ran in Latvian from 2010 to 2014. Photo: Andrejs Zavadskis.

Where it began

2004: a photograph of fireworks over the Riga Radio and Television Tower wins first prize in a competition run by the Latvian State Radio and Television Centre. The Riga Photo Club followed, then a Master Craftsman's Diploma from the Latvian Chamber of Crafts in 2008. In 2009 came a cover and multi-page feature in the UK magazine South Wales YOURWEDDING, and Wedding Photographer of the Year from the Latvian Wedding Photographers Association. March 2025: third place in the Fstoppers international contest "This Was Difficult!".

Riga, today

One photo studio in Riga, where the commercial, advertising, editorial and portrait work begins. A YouTube channel of camera reviews and experiments. More than 7,000 volunteer photographs of Latvia's 2025 School Youth Song and Dance Festival. New stories on blog.zavadskis.eu and new pictures on fotoblog.zavadskis.eu. And ProPortfolio.pro — the platform he founded in 2026 to help photographers, videographers and other creatives find clients and collaborators.
